    How to play

    You are a cat. The Tall One has left you alone in the flat from 9:00 until 17:30. Your research question: does gravity still work? Knock things off tables, shelves and counters. Each thing that hits the floor is a confirmed experiment. Higher drops, breakages and quick combos are worth more science.

    Work through the hypotheses in the top left for big bonuses. Science becomes treats at the end of the day, and treats buy coats and hats.
